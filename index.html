❓ Frequently asked questions

How many kilometers are in a mile?

Exactly 1.609344 kilometers. The international mile is defined as 1,609.344 meters, so to turn miles into kilometers multiply by 1.609344, and to turn kilometers into miles divide by it. For example, 5 miles is 8.04672 kilometers.

How do I convert Fahrenheit to Celsius?

Subtract 32, then divide by 1.8 (the same as multiplying by 5/9). For example, 98.6 °F is (98.6 - 32) / 1.8 = 37 °C. To go the other way, multiply Celsius by 1.8 and add 32. Kelvin is Celsius plus 273.15.

How many pounds are in a kilogram?

About 2.204622622 pounds. The pound is defined as exactly 0.45359237 kilograms, so 1 kilogram is 1 divided by 0.45359237 pounds. An ounce is one sixteenth of a pound, or exactly 28.349523125 grams.

What is a knot?

A knot is one nautical mile per hour. A nautical mile is exactly 1,852 meters, so a knot is 1.852 kilometers per hour, or about 1.151 miles per hour. It is the usual speed unit at sea and in aviation.

How precise are the results?

The converter uses exact definitions for every factor and shows results to 10 significant digits, dropping trailing zeros. Very small or very large results are shown in scientific notation, such as 6.213712e-7 miles for 1 millimeter. Round the result to what your task needs.
